## What I'm looking for

Senior backend or platform engineering roles at product-driven companies and well-funded startups. Strong preference for teams building developer tooling, internal platforms, or data infrastructure. Remote, hybrid, or in-office in Bangalore, Hyderabad, or Pune.

## Target titles

- Senior Backend Engineer
- Senior Platform Engineer
- Senior Infrastructure Engineer
- Senior SRE

Would also consider Staff-level roles where the scope is clearly IC rather than managerial.

## Stack I'm most productive in

- Go and Python for services
- Kubernetes, Terraform, AWS
- Postgres and Redis for storage
- GitHub Actions and ArgoCD for CI/CD

Comfortable reading and modifying Java and TypeScript/Node codebases but wouldn't lead a frontend-heavy role.

## Compensation

- Current CTC: 35 LPA
- Expected CTC: 45 to 55 LPA
- Will consider ESOPs at early-stage startups with strong fundamentals

## Notice period

- 30 days (negotiable with buyout)

## Deal breakers

- Night shifts or permanent US-hours rotation without additional compensation
- Sole infrastructure owner at a seed-stage startup without a clear hiring plan
- 6-day work weeks or no WFH flexibility
- Service-based / body-shopping companies

## What I bring

- 6 years hands-on experience scaling backend systems and developer platforms
- Cost-reduction track record: cut AWS bill 40% at Razorpay without SLA regression
- Built and operated a multi-region Postgres setup handling 15K TPS peak
- Comfortable in ambiguous early-stage scope, also comfortable in scaled-up teams

## Open questions I ask interviewers

- What does the team own end-to-end, and who owns the pieces adjacent to it?
- How much of the work is net-new vs maintenance, and how does that split change over a year?
- What does a week on call actually look like?
- What is the team's WFH and flexible hours policy?
